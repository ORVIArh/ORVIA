# Native full-model GPU diagnostic admission

This packet admits the completed H200 numerical diagnostic for the separately named phosphate v0.3 native-B models. It is a **retrospective operator attestation**, not a job dispatched by the contracts. The complex has 132,935 particles and the solvent has 151,505. The onchain counter records **six comparison cases**: initial, relaxed and post-diagnostic CPU Reference versus CUDA-double energy/force comparisons for each leg. It does not record six molecular-dynamics steps.

`diagnosticOnly` remains true, and scientific acceptance and production readiness remain false. The original limits remain 0.01 kJ/mol absolute energy error, 0.05 kJ/mol/nm maximum force error and 5e-5 relative force L2 error. The diagnostic includes bounded relaxation and ten 0.1fs steps per leg. It does not establish equilibration, energy conservation, thermodynamic sampling, free-energy uncertainty, experimental affinity or full campaign cost.
